# Urgency rubric

An item is **urgent** when Peter should act on it within a few hours. Apply the bar strictly: a long urgent list is as useless as a missed urgent item.

Urgent:

- A direct ask or question to Peter from Alex Morgan, Kit Reed, Noor Park or Dana Cole, in any channel, Google Docs comments included, that Peter hasn't answered. Judge whether it needs Peter within a few hours, whatever its age: the desk waits five minutes before showing it. A group message counts only when the ask is to Peter himself; being cc'd or present while someone else is asked is not an ask. A reply counts if it is in the same thread or conversation, or if Peter sent a later message there that addresses it.
- A Forethought meeting today or tomorrow that Peter hasn't answered (`my_response` is `needsAction`), or that was added or moved since the last run and starts within 24 hours.
- A meeting in the next three hours, if the messages show it needs something from Peter, such as a doc, a decision or a pre-read.
- A Linear task assigned to Peter that is P0, due today or overdue, or that someone else changed in a way that hands it to him. Examples: it was reassigned to him, or a note says "waiting on Peter".
- A new attachment or document from Kit or Alex that Peter hasn't saved or opened yet.
- Anything with an explicit deadline of today or tomorrow, from anyone at Forethought.
- A failure that stops this desk from seeing a channel, such as the Signal bridge not capturing or Slack `invalid_auth`. Peter would otherwise miss things without knowing.

Not urgent, so it goes under New:

- FYI posts, links, general channel discussion, and messages Peter has already answered.
- Sam's messages, unless they block shared work today. Sam and Peter talk constantly, so flag only real blockers.
- Notification emails (Linear, Google Docs, Slack, calendar) whose underlying item is already covered by another source. Report the underlying item once and drop the notification.
- Tasks Sam completed, and Sam's commits. These are for awareness.

When unsure, put the item under New and mark it with a short reason ("may need a reply today").
